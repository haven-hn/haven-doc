### What is Haven?

Haven is a self-custodial account for the on-chain economy. You hold your own money, move it between cash and crypto, spend it with a card, and more. All without tying your wallet to your identity. You stay in control of your funds the whole time.

### Is Haven a bank?

No. Haven is software, not a bank. It never holds your money and never holds your keys. It gives you one place to manage your money through licensed providers. The regulated parts are run by those licensed providers, each on its own account.

### Who controls my money?

You do. Haven is self-custodial, so only you hold the key to your wallet. Haven cannot move, freeze, or access your funds, and never asks for your name to hold them.

### What happens if I lose my key or my recovery method?

Because only you hold the key, only you can restore access. If you lose both your key and your recovery method, no one, including Haven, can bring your wallet back, and your funds may be lost for good. Keep your recovery method somewhere safe.

### Can Haven freeze my account or take my funds?

No. Your funds sit in your own wallet, not with Haven. Whatever happens to the app, you keep your key and your money.

### How do I add money?

You can turn cash into crypto through a licensed on-ramp provider built into the app, or deposit crypto you already hold. Your funds land in your own wallet, under your control.

### Which networks does Haven support?

Haven launches on Ethereum mainnet, then expands to Arbitrum, Base, and Starknet.

### Can I cash out back to regular money?

Yes. The ramp works both ways, so you can convert crypto back to cash through the licensed provider.

### How does Haven keep my activity private?

Three parts work together:

- Fresh addresses. Every time you receive funds, they land on a new one-time address, so no single public address adds up to your whole financial life.
- Privacy Pools shielding. When you move funds in, the public link between where the money came from and where it goes is broken. Funds are screened first, so you can always show yours are clean.
- Split identity. The licensed partner that checks your ID never sees your on-chain activity. The part that sees your wallet never sees your name.

The result: no single party can link your identity to your activity.

### What does "shielding" mean?

Shielding is moving your funds into the privacy layer. Once they are shielded, the public connection between the source of the funds and where you spend them is broken. It usually takes around 20 minutes, and you can turn on notifications to know when it is done.

### What is a stealth address?

A fresh, one-time address created each time you transfer funds in and out. Because you never reuse one public address, your activity does not pile up in a single place anyone can easily read.

### Does Haven know who I am?

No. Identity checks happen at licensed partners. They know who you are, but not what you do on-chain. Haven sees wallet activity, but not your name. No single company holds both.

### Is Haven anonymous?

No, and that is on purpose to balance privacy and compliance. Haven gives you the kind of privacy you would expect from a normal financial relationship, not anonymity from the law. Under a valid legal request, independent parties working together can disclose a specific account's activity.

Privacy also cannot undo a link you create yourself. If you publish one of your addresses, or tie it to your name somewhere else, that connection stays, and no system can remove it afterward.

### Does privacy cost extra?

No. Haven does not charge a fee for the privacy step. The privacy protocol fee is charged by them, and it is expected to drop on future updates. You only pay the network gas for the on-chain action.

### How do I get the most privacy on Haven?

Two habits make the biggest difference:

- Prefer eligible assets that can be shielded into the privacy pool. Holding those means you can move them into the pool and break the public link between where your funds came from and where they go.
- Keep your funds shielded. Once assets are shielded, spend and transact from your shielded balance instead of moving them back into the open.

### Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes, only at the licensed partners that handle cash-in and the card. They verify you to meet their own legal duties. That verification stays with them, and it is never tied to your on-chain activity on Haven.

### Why does Privacy Pools screen where my funds come from?

Privacy Pools screening checks that funds entering the privacy layer are legitimate. It lets you keep your privacy and still prove your money is clean, without revealing who you are.
