### What is Haven?

Haven is a self-custodial neobank for the onchain economy. You hold your own money, move it between cash and crypto, and pay with a card, without tying your identity to your onchain activity.

### Is Haven a bank?

No. Haven is software, not a bank. It never holds your money or your keys. It gives you one place to manage your money through regulated, licensed providers.

### Who controls my money?

You do. Haven is self-custodial, so only you hold the key to your wallet through your passkey. Haven cannot move or access your funds.

### Where can I get support?

Contact us on Telegram at [t.me/haven_support_bot](https://t.me/haven_support_bot) or email [support@haven.hn](mailto:support@haven.hn). For legal or privacy requests, email [legal@haven.hn](mailto:legal@haven.hn).

### How do I add money?

Tap Receive, choose the network and token you’re sending, and send them to the address shown. Buying crypto with cash is coming soon.

### Which networks can I deposit from?

Starknet, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Tron, Solana, and Fuel.

### Which tokens can I deposit?

USDC, USDT, STRK, ETH, and WBTC. Supported tokens vary by network: Ethereum supports USDC, USDT, WBTC, STRK, and ETH; Base supports USDC, WBTC, and ETH; Arbitrum supports USDC and ETH; Polygon supports USDC and ETH (as WETH); Tron supports USDT; Solana supports USDC and USDT; Fuel supports USDC; Starknet supports all listed tokens. The app shows the tokens supported on the network you select.

### What are the deposit limits?

Each network and token has its own minimum and maximum deposit amount. The app shows these limits before you send.

### Is my deposit address fixed? Does it expire?

Always use the address shown in the app when you tap Receive. The address may change between deposits to help keep them private and unlinked. An old address may stop working, so copy the address from the app right before sending. Send only the token on the network shown, or your deposit may not be credited.

### What if I send less or more than the amount shown?

Your deposit may still go through at the rate available when it is processed, so you may receive a different amount than quoted. Sending less than the minimum may mean your deposit is not processed. Send the exact amount shown to keep the quote.

### What if I send the wrong token or use the wrong network?

The deposit won’t be credited automatically. If the token is supported on that network, it may still be processed. Otherwise, contact support to ask about recovery. The address screen tells you which token and network to use. On Polygon, ETH means WETH; POL deposits may not be credited automatically.

### What if I send several deposits at the same time?

Each deposit is matched to your most recent open deposit for that token, preferring the one with the exact amount.

### Can I deposit from an exchange?

Yes, as long as you send the correct token on the correct network to the address shown in the app.

### How long does a deposit take?

Deposits usually arrive in seconds to a few minutes, depending on the network. The app shows an estimate. Your funds must then be shielded before you can use them.

### Are there deposit fees?

Haven covers the network and bridge fees. The shield fee is deducted from your deposit.

### If a deposit fails, how do I get my money back?

Each deposit has a return address, so a failed deposit can be sent back. Contact support if the funds do not return automatically.

### How does Haven keep my activity private?

Every deposit is shielded into a privacy pool, which breaks the public link between where your money came from and where you spend it. Funds are screened first so you can show they are clean. The licensed partner that checks your ID does not see your onchain activity, and the part that sees your wallet does not see your name.

### What does shielding mean?

Shielding moves your funds into the privacy pool. Every deposit is shielded before you can use it, so your balance stays private.

### Why is my deposit locked?

Every deposit must be shielded before you can use it. Until then, it appears as locked: it counts toward your balance but cannot be spent or sent. Tap Shield now to shield it.

### Can I shield more than one asset at a time?

Yes. You can shield multiple assets at the same time.

### How long does shielding take?

After you confirm, an AML check runs first. It usually takes about 30 minutes. We’ll notify you when your funds are ready to use.

### Does privacy cost extra?

Each shield has a privacy fee of 7 STRK, deducted from the deposit. Its value in US dollars varies with the price of STRK, and the app shows it in US dollars before you confirm. You can shield multiple assets at the same time; the fee is charged per shield. Haven does not add an extra fee.

### What happens if my deposit does not pass the AML check?

It cannot be shielded or used in Haven. You can send it back to a wallet of yours. The privacy fee is not refunded, and the network fee for sending it back is deducted from the amount.

### Why are my funds screened before they are shielded?

Screening checks that funds entering the privacy pool are legitimate. It lets you keep your privacy while showing that your money is clean, without revealing who you are.

### Is Haven anonymous?

No. Haven provides the privacy you would expect from a normal financial relationship, not anonymity from the law. Under a valid legal request, licensed providers may disclose a specific account’s activity. Privacy also cannot undo a link you create yourself: if you publish one of your addresses or tie it to your name, that link remains.

### What is a stealth address?

A fresh, one-time address created each time you move funds in or out.

### Does Haven know who I am?

Identity checks happen at licensed partners. They know who you are, but not what you do onchain. Haven sees wallet activity, but not your name. No single company holds both.

### How do I withdraw?

Tap Send, choose Transfer, select a network and token, enter the amount and destination address, then review and confirm with your passkey.

### Which networks can I withdraw to?

Starknet, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Polygon, Tron, Solana, and Fuel. Supported tokens vary by network; the app shows the tokens you can send.

### Why can’t I withdraw? Why are withdrawal options hidden with a zero balance?

You can only send funds that are shielded and ready to use. If none are available, Send is disabled. Deposit and shield your funds first.

### How long does a withdrawal take, and are there fees?

Withdrawals usually arrive in seconds to a few minutes, depending on the network. Haven covers network and bridge fees.

### Can I cash out to regular money?

Coming soon. You’ll be able to convert crypto back to cash through a licensed provider.

### Where is Haven and its card unavailable?

Haven and its card are unavailable to people who are citizens or residents of, or located in: Afghanistan, Belarus, Central African Republic, China, Côte d’Ivoire, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Guinea-Bissau, Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Liberia, Mali, Myanmar, North Korea, Pakistan, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Trinidad and Tobago, Uganda, Ukraine, Vanuatu, Venezuela, Yemen, and Zimbabwe. They are available elsewhere.

### What if my funds come from a sanctioned address?

They will not pass the AML check, so they cannot be shielded or used in Haven. You can send them back to a wallet of yours.

### Can Haven freeze my funds or my card?

Haven cannot freeze or move the funds in your wallet: only you control them. The card issuer can block your card under its own rules. You can freeze your card yourself in the app.

### Can I use a VPN?

No. Using a VPN to get around a country restriction is not allowed.

### Do I need to verify my identity?

You need identity verification to buy or cash out with a licensed partner and to get the card. The partner verifies you to meet its legal duties. That verification is not tied to your full onchain activity on Haven.

### Where can I use my card?

Your Haven card is a virtual Visa card. Add it to Apple Pay or Google Pay and use it anywhere Visa is accepted, subject to the country restrictions above and the card issuer’s rules.

### What can’t I buy with my card?

The card issuer may decline payments for gambling and betting; lotteries and games of chance; guns, ammunition, and other weapons; illegal drugs and controlled substances; prostitution and escort services; counterfeit or gray-market goods and pirated content; Ponzi or pyramid schemes and other deceptive offers; and merchants in sanctioned countries or regions, including Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk. This list is not complete and may change. The virtual card cannot be used to withdraw cash at ATMs.

### What are the card fees and limits?

You can add USDC or USDT to your card. There is no monthly or annual fee. Your first top-up must be at least $10, and the full amount is added to your card balance. Haven covers the $0.30 fee on each card payment. Payments in a currency other than US dollars have a 2% FX fee. There is no crypto conversion fee. The maximum card balance is $100,000 per user.

### What laws and rules apply to my card?

The card is issued by Third National through a card program facilitated by Zypto. The international card terms are governed by the laws of Puerto Rico. Disputes follow the arbitration process in those terms; court proceedings allowed under the agreement generally take place in Puerto Rico, subject to mandatory laws that apply where you live.

### Why was my card application declined?

The card issuer decides who can get a card and may not share the reason. Your Haven account and balance are not affected.

### What if I see a payment I did not make?

Freeze your card right away in the app and contact support.

### What if I lose my phone?

It depends on where your passkey is saved. What matters is where the passkey itself lives, not whether you signed up with email or Google. Most passkeys are saved to your Apple account (iCloud Keychain), Google account (Google Password Manager), or a password manager and sync to your other devices. Sign in to the same account on your new device to access Haven. If your passkey was saved only on the lost device, contact support.

### How do I delete my Haven account?

In the app, go to Profile, tap Delete account, and confirm. Withdraw your balance and wait for any pending operation to finish first. You can also email [legal@haven.hn](mailto:legal@haven.hn) from the address linked to your account. We delete your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it. Deleting your account does not delete your wallet or your onchain assets.
