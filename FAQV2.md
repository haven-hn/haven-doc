### What is Haven?

Haven is a self-custodial neobank for the onchain economy. You hold your own money, move it between cash and crypto, and more (card soon!). All without tying your identity to your onchain activity. You stay in control of your funds the whole time.

### Is Haven a bank?

No. Haven is software, not a bank. It never holds your money and never holds your keys. It gives you one place to manage your money through regulated licensed providers.

### Who controls my money?

You do. Haven is self-custodial, so only you hold the key to your wallet. Haven cannot move, freeze, or access your funds, and never asks for your identity to hold them.

### How do I add money?

You can turn cash into crypto through a licensed on-ramp provider built into the app, or deposit crypto you already hold. Your funds land in your own wallet, under your control.

### Which networks does Haven support?

Haven is live on Ethereum mainnet, expanding to Arbitrum, Base, and Starknet soon.

### Can I cash out back to regular money?

Yes. The ramp works both ways, so you can convert crypto back to cash through a licensed provider of your choice.

### How does Haven keep my activity private?

Three parts work together:

Fresh addresses. Every time you receive funds, they land on a new one-time address, so no single public address adds up to your whole financial life.

Privacy Pools shielding. When you move funds in, the public link between where the money came from and where it goes is broken. Funds are screened first, so you can always show yours are clean.

Split identity. The licensed partner that checks your ID never sees your on-chain activity. The part that sees your wallet never sees your name.

The result: no single party can link your identity to your activity.

### What does "shielding" mean?

Shielding is moving your funds into the privacy layer. Once they are shielded, the public connection between the source of the funds and where you spend them is broken. It usually takes anything between 20 minutes up to a few hours, and you can turn on notifications to know when it is done.

### What is a stealth address?

A fresh, one-time address created each time you transfer funds in and out.

### Does Haven know who I am?

No. Identity checks happen at licensed partners. They know who you are, but not what you do on-chain. Haven sees wallet activity, but not your name. No single company holds both.

### Is Haven anonymous?

Yes. Haven gives you the kind of privacy you would expect from a normal financial relationship. However, it does not mean anonymity from the law. Under a valid legal request, we may work together with the licensed providers to disclose a specific account's activity.

Privacy also cannot undo a link you create yourself. If you publish one of your addresses, or tie it to your name somewhere else, that connection stays, and no system can remove it afterward.

### Does privacy cost extra?

No. Haven does not charge a fee for the privacy step. The privacy protocol charge a small fee for shielding, expected to drop entirely on future updates. You only pay the network gas for the on-chain action.

### How do I get the most privacy on Haven?

Two habits make the biggest difference:

Prefer eligible assets that can be shielded into the privacy pool and keep your funds shielded.

Once assets are shielded, spend and transact from your shielded balance instead of moving them back into the open.

### Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes, at the licensed partners that handle crypto purchase and cash out. They verify you to meet their own legal duties. That verification stays with them, and it is tied to unique addresses for each transaction, not to your entire on-chain activity on Haven.

### Why does Privacy Pools screen where my funds come from?

Privacy Pools screening checks that funds entering the privacy layer are legitimate. It lets you keep your privacy and still prove your money is clean, without revealing who you are.
